## Summary

`BudgetAuthority._validate_revision()` iterated `dimensions` /
`enforcement_rules` without ever requiring the exact `tuple` container
type, so a caller-supplied mutable `list` was accepted rather than
rejected. It also never checked `dimension_ref` / `rule_ref` uniqueness
within one revision, nor that every rule's `dimension_ref` matched a
dimension declared in that same revision -- allowing duplicate/ambiguous
identifiers and orphan rule dimensions to publish and later be enforced.

`publish_policy_revision()` now fails closed with stable machine-readable
codes:

- `BUDGET_POLICY_REVISION_INVALID` -- list-shaped `dimensions` or
  `enforcement_rules` (reused: same code already covers other structural
  shape defects on this dataclass);
- `BUDGET_POLICY_REVISION_DIMENSION_DUPLICATE` -- repeated
  `dimension_ref` within one revision;
- `BUDGET_POLICY_REVISION_RULE_DUPLICATE` -- repeated `rule_ref` within
  one revision;
- `BUDGET_POLICY_REVISION_RULE_DIMENSION_UNDECLARED` -- a rule's
  `dimension_ref` does not match any dimension declared in the same
  revision.

No change was made to `reserve()`, `_require_identical_replay()`, policy
selection/supersession (`_current_policy_revision`), or any other
behavior. F-001 and F-002 are untouched, per Non-Goals.
