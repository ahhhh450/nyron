## Objective

Implement the smallest production-quality Human Interaction Owner foundation that establishes:

1. immutable `HumanRequest` canonical truth bound to Project / Workspace / pinned policy context by reference;
2. immutable Human-Interaction-owned `ResponsePolicyRevision` sufficient to preserve the frozen aggregation contract without defining a product role taxonomy;

## Contract Constraints

1. `HumanRequest` identity is stable and immutable; exact replay may be idempotent, conflicting redefinition fails closed.
2. A request binds exact Project / Workspace / pinned policy-context and response-policy references needed by D-009; those foreign refs are consumed, not re-owned.
3. `ResponsePolicyRevision` is immutable once referenced by a request. Conflicting redefinition under the same identity fails closed.
4. A rejected ingress candidate must leave no canonical `HumanResponse` truth behind.
5. An accepted `HumanResponse` is immutable and permanently bound to exactly one HumanRequest and its accepted semantic payload/evidence references.
6. Same stable response identity + same semantic payload/evidence binding is deterministic/idempotent. Same stable response identity + different semantic payload or binding fails closed and leaves original truth unchanged.
7. Different response identities from the same principal are handled only according to the request's pinned `ResponsePolicyRevision`; do not silently count duplicates.
8. Aggregation consumes accepted responses only. Rejected ingress material must never satisfy quorum/cardinality or appear in canonical `HumanDecisionEvidence`.
9. Once the request reaches `SATISFIED`, its canonical `HumanDecisionEvidence` must be immutable and deterministically linked to the exact accepted response set and pinned response policy that produced it.
10. `EXPIRED`, `CANCELLED`, `SUPERSEDED`, response acceptance and `SATISFIED` transitions must serialize through one owner-local transactional boundary so only one valid terminal truth wins.
11. A late response after a terminal state fails closed for canonical acceptance in this slice.
12. No Human Interaction method/return type/side effect may create or mutate Runtime, Capability, Recovery, Effect, PWP or Distribution canonical state.
13. Preserve the standing synchronous SQLite single-writer assumption. Do not introduce pools, concurrent raw writers, process-distributed authority or background workers.
14. Keep the implementation minimal. Do not pre-build concrete transport/UI/runtime integration.
