## Objective

```text
ACTIVE
  -> REVOKE_REQUESTED
  -> FENCED       when cessation/no-future-continuation is exactly proven

  -> UNKNOWN      when neither exact completion nor exact cessation can be proven
```

Also support the current implementation's strongly provable non-dispatch case:


only when the operation is still canonically PREPARED and current implementation evidence proves external mutation has not begun. PREPARED/FENCED must never be described as semantic retry clearance.

### B. Revoke resolution

Provide the smallest exact resolution/report path corresponding to frozen `ReportRevokeResult` / effect evidence handling.

For `REVOKE_REQUESTED`:

- exact external completion evidence matching immutable target/payload -> `COMPLETED` with normal immutable completion evidence;
- exact adapter-specific cessation/no-future-continuation evidence -> `FENCED`;
- absent, mismatched, substituted or ambiguous evidence when cessation cannot be proven -> `UNKNOWN`;
- never infer FENCED merely from Capability/Lease revoke, timeout, absence of acknowledgement, or caller assertion.

For this concrete trusted bounded filesystem effect, use only the minimum deterministic evidence that is actually provable under the current synchronous/no-background model. Do not invent a generalized cancellation-provider protocol.

### C. Durable fence evidence

A `FENCED` result must have durable exact evidence sufficient to audit **why future continuation is known stopped/non-dispatchable**.

Use the smallest schema surface needed, e.g. an immutable operation-owned fence/revoke evidence field if required. Do not overload completion evidence: completion evidence proves an external consequence; fence evidence proves cessation/non-continuation and must remain semantically distinct.

Any new evidence field must be immutable after first commit and machine-checkably constrained to the appropriate state.

### D. FENCED is not retry clearance

Machine-check/test all of the following:

- FENCED cannot transition back to PREPARED/ACTIVE/REVOKE_REQUESTED;
- FENCED cannot be executed again under the same operation_ref;
- no API/result/evidence field introduced by this Task says or implies `safe_to_retry` / semantic retry clearance;
- FENCED may coexist conceptually with historical outcome uncertainty; do not fabricate `COMPLETED` or `no_effect` from fencing alone;
- no automatic creation/admission of a replacement EffectOperation follows FENCED.
