- `FENCED != retry clearance`.
- `FENCED != no prior consequence`.
- `UNKNOWN != zero usage` and `UNKNOWN != no external consequence`.
- Effect lifecycle/activity and historical outcome remain separate.
- Historical outcome refinement remains monotonic `UNKNOWN -> PARTIAL -> KNOWN`.
- Recovery may collect evidence but may not write Effect or Accounting truth directly.
- Accounting owns BudgetReservation, UsageFact / UsageAdjustmentFact, settlement and reconciliation truth.
- BudgetReservation must be `RESERVED` before any future billable dispatch.
- EffectOperation must be `PREPARED` before any future outbound dispatch.

- Timeout, disconnect, cancel request, socket close, local error, or missing response are not proof of no consequence and not retry clearance.
- No new Owner boundary or Architecture semantics may be invented.

- different semantic operation reusing an existing protected idempotency key within the same declared namespace/scope fails closed;
- external operation/request ID is set-once and conflicting later identity fails closed;
- restart persistence preserves all bindings and replay/conflict behavior.

### D. Accounting reconciliation

Implement the smallest Accounting-owned API/command and persistence behavior needed for Provider billable ambiguity.

Required behavior:

- exact reservation can enter `RECONCILING` from accepted Provider `UNKNOWN/PARTIAL` ambiguity evidence;
- entering/replaying the same reconciliation identity is idempotent;
- conflicting reconciliation identity/evidence fails closed;
- `RECONCILING` grants no new spend authority;
- ambiguous usage is never silently released or recorded as zero;
- stable Provider request/line-item/callback identity maps deterministically to immutable UsageFact source identity;
- accepted authoritative evidence may later resolve reconciliation to the already-frozen valid accounting terminal outcome (`COMMITTED` or `RELEASED`) only according to existing Accounting semantics;
