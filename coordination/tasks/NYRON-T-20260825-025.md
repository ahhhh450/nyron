5. verify required consumptive/non-consumptive input availability;
6. deterministically bind consumptive Trigger / REQUIRED_NEXT Deliveries;
7. snapshot REQUIRED_LATEST / OPTIONAL_LATEST without consuming them;
8. atomically mark consumptive Deliveries bound to the new `activation_ref`;
9. create one immutable Activation with exact bindings;
10. persist the minimal canonical Activation-created evidence required by the accepted plan/current kernel conventions, without inventing a generalized event subsystem.
