## Product Direction — Binding Requirement

Nyron's user-facing nodes are **module assembly nodes**, not a visualization of every Runtime/canonical object.

Target layering:

```text
Module
  ↓
ProductNodeDefinition
  ↓
NodeInstance + Ports + Connections
  ↓
VisualWorkflowRevision
  ↓ deterministic compile/project
GraphRevision
  ↓
Execution Runtime
```

A Product Node may package several lower-level canonical mechanisms. For example an LLM Node may internally consume Provider/Credential/Network/Accounting/Effect, while a Human Approval Node may consume HumanRequest/HumanResponse/HumanDecisionEvidence plus Runtime suspension/resume. Do not expose those lower-level objects as separate Product Nodes merely because they exist.
