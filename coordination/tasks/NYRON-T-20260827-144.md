## Required Semantics

Preserve two independent canonical facts for an EffectOperation:

1. activity/conflict lifecycle — whether work is PREPARED, ACTIVE, REVOKE_REQUESTED, FENCED, COMPLETED, UNKNOWN as already defined by current authority;
2. historical consequence knowledge — a separate owner-controlled dimension sufficient to distinguish at least:
   - `UNKNOWN`: relevant historical consequence cannot be established;
   - `PARTIAL`: some consequence/evidence is known but complete terminal consequence is not yet established;
   - `KNOWN`: historical consequence is sufficiently established for the owning effect contract.

The implementation may choose exact field/API names, but MUST NOT reinterpret `FENCED` as semantic retry clearance.

Required rules:

- existing bounded-write semantics and tests remain valid;
- `FENCED` may coexist with historical `UNKNOWN` or `PARTIAL` where future effect families need that representation;
- `COMPLETED` must not silently imply a generic provider/browser accounting meaning beyond its concrete effect contract;
- historical outcome transitions are owner-controlled, fail-closed, durable, replay-safe and immutable except for explicitly authorized monotonic refinement;
- no caller may set historical truth through a generic raw update;
- retry/redispatch admission is NOT added in this Task unless required solely to prevent an existing path from treating FENCED as clearance;
- no Provider, Browser, Remote Worker, Process, Network, or Workspace adapter implementation in this Task;
- no Accounting ownership transfer; Accounting truth remains owned by Accounting.
