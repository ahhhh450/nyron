### B. ACTIVE crash / recovery truthfulness

Once `ACTIVE` is durable, recovery must not guess whether an external mutation happened.

- exact live external evidence matching immutable operation target/payload -> `COMPLETED` with exact completion evidence;
- absent target/evidence -> `UNKNOWN`;
- mismatched bytes/hash/target/provenance -> `UNKNOWN`;
- substituted/ambiguous target -> `UNKNOWN`;
- no path from `ACTIVE` back to `PREPARED`;
- no fabricated completion evidence;
- no automatic retry or second mutation attempt after ambiguous ACTIVE recovery.
